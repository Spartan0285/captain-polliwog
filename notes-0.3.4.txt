Captain Polliwog 0.3.4

New

- A sidebar for your tabs, on Shift-Command-L. Favicons, titles, and a
  close cross on the row you are pointing at.
- A Reader button appears in the address bar when a page is worth
  reading that way, and not when it is not.
- A history window on Command-Y, searchable, with the last two thousand
  pages.
- Close every tab and you land on Top Sites and your favourites rather
  than an empty window. If you would rather not have Top Sites, there is
  now a switch for it in Preferences.
- The keyboard shortcuts from Safari: Command-1 to 9 for tabs,
  Command-0, Command-Shift-[ and ] to move between them, Control-Tab,
  Command-O to open a file, Command-J for downloads, and a Develop menu
  with Option-Command-U for the page source.

Fixed

- A crash while reading webmail. Style invalidation walked a stack of
  ancestor elements and, in a case Gmail produces, popped it past the
  bottom and read an element that was not there. Found by leaving pages
  running for ten minutes at a time; the browser had been closing them
  too quickly to notice.
- Cookies on Tiger. The engine asked for a property of a cookie that
  10.4 has never had, which stopped it partway through the list, so
  whether a cookie reached a page depended on where it happened to sit.
  This does not give Tiger HttpOnly protection - that system cannot
  represent the flag at all - but it no longer loses cookies over it.
- The address bar is the same colour as the rest of the toolbar again,
  on both Tiger and Leopard, including the strip behind the text while
  you are typing in it.
- The offer to download VLC for PowerPC works again. VideoLAN moved that
  file behind two redirects to a mirror; it now comes from the master
  host directly.

Faster

- About six per cent on Speedometer 3.1, from four fixes to
  JavaScriptCore's baseline JIT: arithmetic profiling that was being
  written and never read, inline cache sizes that were wrong for this
  processor, the "in" operator compiled properly for every kind of
  array, and a call into libm that was corrupting our own stack frame.
- The engine is now chosen by your processor as well as your system, so
  a G4 gets the build made for a G4.

Known

- You cannot sign in to a Google account. Google's sign-in needs BigInt,
  which this engine does not have and cannot be given without a newer
  one. Work on that newer engine is under way and is a long road.
- Some large sites use a lot of memory - Vimeo can reach a gigabyte on a
  page left open. Keep an eye on it if your Mac has 512MB or less.
